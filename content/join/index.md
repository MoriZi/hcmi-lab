              <h2 class="join-h2">About the Lab</h2>
              <p>The Human-Centered Machine Intelligence (HCMI) Lab at Toronto Metropolitan University develops next-generation AI that places people at the center of intelligent systems. We are always looking for outstanding students and researchers passionate about building trustworthy, responsible, and human-centered AI.</p>
              <p>Our research spans both theoretical foundations and real-world applications — combining machine learning, information retrieval, large language models, knowledge graphs, and responsible AI to build systems that are ethical, transparent, scalable, and beneficial to society. We regularly publish in leading AI, ML, IR, and Data Mining venues.</p>
              <p>We welcome applicants interested in one or more of the research areas listed here. If your interests align, we'd love to hear from you.</p>

                    <li>Why you want to join the HCMI Lab</li>
                    <li>Research interests</li>
                    <li>Previous research experience</li>
                    <li>Programming &amp; machine learning experience</li>
                    <li>Mathematical background</li>
                    <li>Publications, scholarships, or awards (if any)</li>
