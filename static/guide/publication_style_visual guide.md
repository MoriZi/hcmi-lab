**STYLE PROMPT — HCMI Academic Research Visuals**

Create a refined, minimalist scientific vector illustration for a university research laboratory website focused on human-centered machine intelligence, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction.

**Visual style:** Contemporary academic editorial design, minimalist scientific infographic, precise geometric vector artwork, elegant research-journal aesthetic, clean Swiss-inspired layout.

**Color palette:** Predominantly near-white and cool off-white backgrounds, very pale icy blue, powder blue, muted steel blue, soft slate blue, and restrained deep navy accents. Low-saturation colors, subtle tonal variations, delicate transparency, and gentle overlapping shapes. Avoid saturated colors.

**Graphic language:** Thin, precise blue linework; clean geometric forms; translucent circles; subtle network connections; small data points; abstract mathematical diagrams; elegant analytical curves; restrained grid structures; simplified scientific symbols. Every element should communicate a meaningful research concept.

**Composition:** Balanced, spacious, horizontally oriented composition, generous negative space, carefully controlled visual hierarchy, crisp edges, subtle rectangular framing when appropriate, and a clear focal point. Keep the illustration visually light and uncluttered.

**Rendering:** Flat vector illustration with extremely subtle depth, delicate gradients only when necessary, consistent line weights, precise alignment, understated technical details, and polished professional art direction. It should look like a carefully art-directed illustration from a high-quality academic journal or a university research laboratory's visual identity.

**Background:** Clean white or very pale cool gray, with no environmental scene, no photographic textures, and no unnecessary decoration.

**Overall mood:** Intelligent, calm, trustworthy, analytical, human-centered, sophisticated, and academically credible.

The final artwork must feel like part of one coherent visual identity across a collection of research illustrations. Prioritize clarity, conceptual accuracy, visual consistency, and professional graphic design over decorative complexity.


**Negative Prompt:**

Photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic surfaces, futuristic sci-fi aesthetics, neon colors, purple-pink gradients, cyberpunk, glowing circuitry, excessive shadows, dramatic lighting, dark backgrounds, complex textures, stock illustration clichés, generic AI brain icons, decorative robots, cartoon characters, hand-drawn sketchiness, visual clutter, excessive detail, thick outlines, oversaturated colors, random floating objects, inconsistent perspective, unnecessary text, gibberish typography, watermarks, logos, and inconsistent visual styles.
